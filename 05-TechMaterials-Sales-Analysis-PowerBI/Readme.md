## 💡 Key Insights
- Electronics generate about 62% of revenue, against 38% for Accessories.
- South and West are the strongest regions in sales.
- Nearly 1 in 4 orders is cancelled, representing about €550K in lost revenue.
- East has the highest cancellation rate (close to 30%), while North has the lowest (under 20%).
- Cancellations peak at the end of the year, especially in November and December.

## ✅ Recommendations
- Investigate the causes of cancellations (delivery delays, stock-outs, payment issues) to reduce the 24.6% rate.
- Prioritize actions in the East region, where cancellations are highest.
- Prepare for the year-end peak with better stock planning and logistics.
- Keep investing in Electronics while growing Accessories sales.
